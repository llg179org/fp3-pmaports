Two-sided dump of the vMPM region (RPM MSG RAM 0x60000 + 0x1d4, 0x48 bytes),
read through /dev/mem with a Python mmap on both sides, phone idle.

Layout, from the driver's own MPM_REGISTER macro with 96 pins (stride 3):
  0x1d4 TIMER0   0x1d8 TIMER1
  0x1dc ENABLE0..2      0x1e8 FALLING0..2
  0x1f4 RISING0..2      0x200 POLARITY0..2
  0x20c STATUS0..2      0x218 is past the array (adjacent MSG RAM)

pmOS mainline, debug-int/7.1.3 with the vMPM timer patch:
  0x1d4: 46683198 00000000 08001000 00080008
  0x1e4: 00000020 00000000 00000008 00000020
  0x1f4: 00000000 00000008 00000020 00001000
  0x204: 00080000 00000000 00000000 00000000
  0x214: 00000000 df2763c5

Ubuntu Touch oracle, 4.9.218-perf, two reads 5 s apart:
  0x1d4: e27f02f8 00000000 0c001004 c0280008
  0x1e4: 01000020 00000000 c0000008 00000020
  0x1f4: 04000000 c4020008 00000020 00001004
  0x204: 00080020 00000000 00000000 00000000
  0x214: 00000000 df2f63c5
  0x1d4: 00000000 00000000 ...   (TIMER0 back to zero 5 s later)

Reading: structurally identical. Every difference in the register words is the
oracle having more wakeup pins enabled - it wakes on more GPIOs. There is no
extra handshake word, no flag and no cookie that mainline is failing to write.
The only behavioural difference is TIMER0 returning to zero on the oracle, i.e.
the value is consumed.

Same boot, oracle lpm_stats and RPM master stats:
  [system] system-pc  success 10168, failed 611, total 117.469 s
  APSS numshutdowns 0x2619 = 9753, active_cores 0x1, xo_count 0
  MPSS numshutdowns 0x2b8 = 696, xo_count 0x29c, active_cores 0x0
The system-pc success count and the APSS shutdown count track each other, which
is the relationship that is entirely absent on mainline.
