☠️ Read the dispersion before the means. The per-sample standard deviation is
about 60 mA in every leg - the load is bursty, not steady - so the standard
error of a 100-sample mean is around 6 mA, which is larger than the 4.5 mA
difference being claimed. The two groups do not overlap, which is suggestive,
but two legs per arm cannot establish it. More legs, appended below as they run.

--- correction, and a split into two sets -----------------------------------

☠️ The five legs above were described as "panel blanked". They were not. The
device-side runner wrote 4 to fb0/blank and moved on; it never checked, and on a
fresh boot the compositor still holds DRM master for a few seconds after
`systemctl stop greetd` returns and undoes the blank as fast as it is written.
The gated script caught it the first time it ran:

    idle-leg: DSI-1 dpms is 'On', not Off

Their currents (155-164 mA) also sit squarely in the panel-on range measured by
hand earlier (151-163 mA on, 142 mA off), which is the independent confirmation.

So the five legs above are a **panel-on** set, internally consistent and still a
valid A/B against each other:

  FIX  -160.6   FIX2 -158.2   FIX3 -164.3      mean -161.0
  CTL  -154.4   CTL2 -155.4                    mean -154.9

and everything from CTL3 onwards is a **panel-off** set, run through
idle-leg.sh, which blanks in a retry loop until dpms actually reads Off and
restores the charger input on every exit path. Do not compare across the two
sets; the panel is worth ~10 mA.

--- the panel-off set, and it reverses the sign -------------------------------

  CTL3 mean_uA=-131107 n=200 settle=300s
  FIX4 mean_uA=-115864 n=200 settle=300s
  CTL4 mean_uA=-129848 n=200 settle=300s
  FIX5 mean_uA=-122220 n=200 settle=300s

  FIX legs: -115.9, -122.2 mA   (mean -119.0, spread 6.3)
  CTL legs: -131.1, -129.8 mA   (mean -130.5, spread 1.3)

  The arms do not overlap - the closest pair is 122.2 against 129.8, a 7.6 mA
  gap - so with the panel genuinely off the genpd fix SAVES about 11.5 mA of
  130, roughly 9 %.

That is the opposite sign from the panel-on set, and the mechanism is the same
one either way: with the display refreshing at 65 Hz the idle windows are too
short for the deep states to repay their entry cost, so the fixed kernel pays
transitions for nothing; with the display off the windows are long enough and
the deeper state wins. Which also means the panel-on set was never measuring
"does the fix help" - it was measuring "does it help while something wakes the
CPU 65 times a second", and the answer to that is no.

☠️ The lesson to carry: the confound was not noise, it was a different regime.
Averaging more samples would never have found it.
